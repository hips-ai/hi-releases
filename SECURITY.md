# Security

Report a vulnerability in `hi`, its installers or its releases privately, never in a public issue: email security@hips.com. HIPS's Vulnerability Disclosure Policy at https://hips.com/security says how reports are handled, and https://hips.com/.well-known/security.txt publishes the same address.

Please include what `hi --version` prints, your system, and the steps that show the problem. Leave out API keys, sign-in tokens, prompts and the contents of your files: a request id (`req_...`) is enough for HIPS to find a request.

Fixes ship in a new release of `hi`. `hi update` installs the newest one.
